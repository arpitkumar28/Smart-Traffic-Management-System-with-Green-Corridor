# Project Submission: GreenFlow AI

## Project Title
**GreenFlow AI: Autonomous Emergency Priority & Traffic Orchestration System**

## Problem Statement
Every year, thousands of lives are lost because ambulances are stuck in traffic. Traditional traffic light systems are "dumb"—they operate on fixed timers and cannot react to emergency vehicles or real-time congestion spikes. This leads to increased emergency response times, higher CO2 emissions, and city-wide gridlock.

## Solution
GreenFlow AI is an end-to-end Smart City solution that uses AI to orchestrate traffic signals in real-time. 
- **Green Corridor Automation**: Automatically detects emergency vehicles and clears a "Green Corridor" by synchronizing signals at **Metro Junction**, **Hospital Road**, **Civic Center**, and **South Park**.
- **AI Predictive Analytics**: Uses historical and real-time data to predict congestion up to 30 minutes in advance with actionable recommendations.
- **Impact Dashboard**: A high-tech interface for city authorities to monitor network health, emergency response, and CO2 savings.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Recharts, Leaflet.
- **Mobile**: Flutter (Material 3) - Home, Live Map, SOS, Alerts, Statistics.
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python), WebSockets, Supabase.
- **AI Engine**: Predictive traffic modeling and adaptive signal synchronization logic.

## 🚀 Demo Features
1. **Intelligent Live Map**: Real-time signal status (🟢, 🟡, 🔴) for 4 major intersections.
2. **Emergency Simulation**: One-tap "🚑 Simulate Ambulance" button that triggers dynamic route clearing and instant priority corridor activation.
3. **AI Traffic Prediction Panel**: Actionable AI output for **Civic Center** showing 92% confidence and recommended signal extensions (+12s).
4. **Analytics**: Live charts for Traffic Volume, Waiting Time, Emergency Response Time, and CO₂ Saved.
