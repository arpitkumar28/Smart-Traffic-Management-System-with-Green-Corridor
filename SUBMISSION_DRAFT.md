# Project Submission: GreenFlow AI

## Project Title
**GreenFlow AI: Autonomous Emergency Priority & Traffic Orchestration System**

## Problem Statement
Every year, thousands of lives are lost because ambulances are stuck in traffic. Traditional traffic light systems are "dumb"—they operate on fixed timers and cannot react to emergency vehicles or real-time congestion spikes. This leads to increased emergency response times, higher CO2 emissions, and city-wide gridlock.

## Solution
GreenFlow AI is an end-to-end Smart City solution that uses AI to orchestrate traffic signals in real-time. 
- **Mission Critical Command Center**: A government-grade, high-performance UI designed for real-time city operations, inspired by Tesla Fleet Command and Air Traffic Control systems.
- **Green Corridor Automation**: Automatically detects emergency vehicles and clears a "Green Corridor" by synchronizing signals at major junctions.
- **AI Predictive Analytics**: Uses historical and real-time data to predict congestion up to 30 minutes in advance with actionable recommendations.
- **Impact Dashboard**: Real-time monitoring of network health, emergency response metrics, and CO2 savings through advanced data visualization.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Framer Motion, Recharts, Leaflet.
- **Mobile**: Flutter (Material 3) - Home, Live Map, SOS, Alerts, Statistics.
- **Backend**: FastAPI (Python), WebSockets, Supabase.
- **AI Engine**: Predictive traffic modeling and adaptive signal synchronization logic.

## 🚀 Demo Features
1. **Global Command Center**: A centralized operations hub with a real-time city grid, AI intelligence feeds, and network health monitoring.
2. **Emergency Simulation & Mode**: A "one-tap" bypass that puts the entire dashboard into **Emergency Mode**, activating red-alert UI states and clearing the Green Corridor.
3. **AI Traffic Intelligence Panel**: Real-time AI recommendations with confidence scoring (e.g., "Extend Signal Cycle +12s").
4. **Anakin Wire Intelligence**: A live intelligence feed processing road closures, weather alerts, and emergency notifications.
5. **Advanced Analytics**: Mission-critical charts for Traffic Flux, Emergency Response Optimization, and Fuel/CO₂ Savings.
